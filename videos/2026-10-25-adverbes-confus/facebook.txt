« I haven't seen her lately. » 🇬🇧

Cette phrase veut dire « je ne l'ai pas vue en retard » ? Non !

Certains adverbes anglais se ressemblent mais n'ont pas du tout le même sens.

3 paires qui piègent :

1️⃣ Late = en retard / Lately = ces derniers temps
👉 We are always late for school.
👉 I haven't seen Larissa lately.

2️⃣ Especially = surtout / Specially = spécialement
👉 I love food, especially Japanese food.
👉 My dress was specially made for me.

3️⃣ Near = près / Nearly = presque
👉 Is there a bank near the grocery?
👉 I have nearly done my homework.
